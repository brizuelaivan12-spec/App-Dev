# Stranger Things 
**Stranger Things** is a popular *science fiction* and *horror* series created by **The Duffer Brothers**.  
It was first released on **Netflix** in **2016** and quickly became one of the most-loved shows around the world.

## Main Plot
The story takes place in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, during the 1980s.  
When a young boy named *Will Byers* goes missing, his friends discover a mysterious girl with supernatural powers **Eleven** who helps them uncover secrets about a hidden dimension called **the Upside Down**.

### Main Characters 
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – One of Will’s best friends  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s police chief  
- **Joyce Byers (Winona Ryder)** – Will’s mother  
- **Dustin, Lucas, and Max** – Members of the group who fight the creatures from the Upside Down

### Favorite Quote
> “Friends don’t lie.” *Eleven*
